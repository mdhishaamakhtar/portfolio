## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7 (static output) + Tailwind CSS v4, TypeScript strict, ESLint + Prettier, Vitest. Deployed to GitHub Pages at hishaam.dev by .github/workflows/deploy.yml, which runs the quality gate before publishing dist/. Social card, icons and portrait are rendered at build time (satori, resvg, sharp).

## Users

- People who heard Hishaam's name (from Harf, from Groww, from a talk or a repo) and want to know who he is in under a minute.
- Recruiters, collaborators, and engineers checking his backend work and side projects.
- Harf's audience: people curious about the engineer half of the two people behind Harf Studios.

## Product Purpose

Personal site of Md Hishaam Akhtar. It says who he is now: a backend engineer at Groww and the co-founder of Harf Studios with Vaniya. It lets visitors reach his work, projects, writing, research, résumé, and contact in a few seconds.

## Positioning

An engineer who builds high-scale backend systems by day and, with Vaniya, is making Urdu fun to learn through Harf. He came to Urdu through Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan and the Sabri Brothers. Values: ideas that question norms, tools that quietly empower, engineering that is inclusive by design.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A single-page site plus a generated OG image, sitemap, robots, manifest, and 404 page.
- Static hosting only (GitHub Pages). Custom domain hishaam.dev, with HTTPS enforced.
- No AI-for-addiction framing any more. That was an older interest, not his current focus.

## Brand Commitments

- The pastel mint green (#e3fcec family, with the deep green #386641) is his colour and must stay the mainstay of the palette.
- He likes the Cormorant Garamond serif voice and wants to keep its spirit.
- Simple and elegant, like the original site: one calm column, no decorative systems. He rejected a Mughal album-page direction ("no Islamic/Mughal motifs") and an engineering-pad direction ("too technical, too much going on").
- Light mint only. No dark mode.
- Urdu appears only in the Harf context. Vaniya Adeeb is credited in the Harf entry, not in his intro line.
- No book cover or product photo: it's his site, and Harf is one entry under Work.
- He is bi and proud of it (the old site showed a pride heart). Keep it visible and unforced.
- Two lines he wrote must survive verbatim in spirit:
  - "ideas that question norms and tools that quietly empower"
  - "Above all, I believe engineering should be inclusive by design: thoughtful, humane, and built to serve those most often left out."
- The copy should sound like a person, not like an AI.

## Product Principles

1. Who he is now, first: an engineer, and the co-founder of Harf.

3. Warm and human: inclusive by design, in tone as well as in markup.
